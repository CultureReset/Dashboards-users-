The concept in plain English

A customer page is just one surface over a deeper business system.

Ghost / CyberCheck is not just a landing page tool. It is a modular business foundation. The business controls data, modules, providers, permissions, automation, and public outputs from one place.

The permanent layer

The foundation is what makes the whole thing modular.

If the foundation is not right, every page, module, provider, and automation becomes spaghetti. Click the stages below.

Identity & Tenancy

Organizations, businesses, locations, users, roles, customers, agents, devices, and ownership boundaries.

Canonical Data

Structured menus, pricing, availability, reviews, events, customers, media, policies, bookings, products and more.

App / Package System

Installable modules that reuse the same foundation instead of creating isolated silos.

Capability Registry

What the system can do: reservation.create, availability.search, review.respond, message.send, etc.

Provider Adapters

Connects business capabilities to actual vendors, APIs, feeds or manual data sources.

Verification & Ledger

Approvals, evidence, action logs, usage records and trusted results.

Build flow

How a developer should think about building it.

This is the step-by-step product flow behind what the page is showing.

Create the workspace

Business, locations, users, roles, permissions and ownership boundaries are created first.

Define canonical data models

Menus, events, pricing, availability, inventory, reviews, packages, forms, customers and any business-specific entities must be structured.

Install app modules

Menu, Booking, Events, Reviews, Loyalty, Song Requests, CRM, Messaging, Availability, Directory Profile, Widget and more.

Map capabilities to providers

Example: “Book a Table” maps to reservation.create and can route to Resy, OpenTable, Toast, or a manual booking workflow.

Render public surfaces

Link-page, scroll-page, full website, QR page, widgets, directory outputs and future interfaces all consume the same foundation.

Set automation, approvals and publishing rules

Define what updates automatically, what needs approval, and what gets published where.

Keep everything replaceable

The business should be able to change UI, modules, or providers later without rebuilding the whole platform.

Best practical product rule: keep the business data and capabilities independent from the page design. The page is a surface, not the source of truth.
