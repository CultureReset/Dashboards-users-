The clean strategic framing

You structure the business world. Infrastructure providers move and execute it. CyberCheck can publish its structured facts and business actions through MCP/API, while Cloudflare or another provider can host, route, cache, execute, secure, or monetize access. That makes Cloudflare a possible infrastructure partner underneath you — not a replacement for your input layer.

FAQ

Simple answers for customers.

What is the difference between an App and a Plugin?

An App adds functionality inside CyberCheck. A Plugin connects CyberCheck to an outside service the business already uses. For example, Booking can be an App; Gmail can be a Plugin.

Do I have to replace my current software?

No. The core idea is to connect what you already use and make it communicate through one operating layer.

What if the app I need does not exist?

CyberCheck searches existing apps, plugins, automations and components first. If the capability is still missing, the AI builder can use Grok Build to generate the missing pieces and install them into the workspace.

Can I really call or text my dashboard?

That is part of the intended interface. Phone and SMS become alternative clients for the same permissioned business data and actions used by the visual dashboard.

Is Cloudflare the database?

No. Cloudflare can provide infrastructure for agents, MCP, containers, edge execution, state and agent-friendly content delivery. CyberCheck's business database remains the structured input and operating layer.
