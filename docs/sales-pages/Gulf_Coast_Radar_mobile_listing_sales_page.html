How it works

Keep the website feel. Upgrade what is behind it.

01 — EXISTING BUSINESS

Start with what already exists

The current website, photos, business listings, booking pages, calendars and other public information provide the starting point.

02 — STRUCTURED PROFILE

Organize the business by section

Hours, pricing, services, inventory, availability, menus, captains, amenities, policies, events and more become reusable business data.

03 — LIVE PAGE

Display it like a real website

The listing becomes a complete responsive page with the sections that actually apply to that business — including live-style booking and availability interfaces.
